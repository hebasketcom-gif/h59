About Dr. Shana Parween

Dr. Shana Parween is a dedicated General Dentist who approaches oral healthcare with professionalism, precision, and genuine empathy. She believes that a healthy smile is integral to overall well-being, prioritizing clear communication and active listening to understand each patient's individual needs.

Her clinical ethos centers on providing comprehensive routine care, preventive maintenance, and restorative solutions in a soothing environment. By combining modern healthcare practices with patient education, Dr. Shana empowers individuals to take proactive charge of their long-term oral health.

Professional Philosophy

Four guiding pillars that define Dr. Shana Parween's patient interactions and daily clinical practice.

01

Patient-Centered Care

Every individual brings unique concerns, medical histories, and expectations. Listening actively and customizing care around the patient's well-being is fundamental to building lasting trust.

02

Comfortable Dental Experiences

Dental anxiety is a common barrier to essential care. Creating a tranquil, reassuring environment with gentle techniques ensures patients feel relaxed and valued at every appointment.

03

Preventive Oral Healthcare

Proactive maintenance always yields superior long-term outcomes compared to reactive treatment. Emphasizing early intervention preserves natural tooth structure whenever possible.

04

Professional & Ethical Care

Upholding strict ethical benchmarks, transparent explanations, and evidence-based clinical practices ensures that patients receive sound recommendations tailored purely to their health.
